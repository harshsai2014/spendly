Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you register, along with the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as login timestamps, to help keep your account secure.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the core features of Spendly: logging expenses, generating category breakdowns, and showing spending summaries over time. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and are only accessible through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on limited third-party services for functionality such as font delivery or hosting infrastructure. These providers only process the minimum data necessary to deliver their service and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at support@spendly.app.
